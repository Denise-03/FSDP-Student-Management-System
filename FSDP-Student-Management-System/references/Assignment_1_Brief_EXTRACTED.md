1. OBJECTIVE
This assignment aims to allow students to apply the Scrum framework to solve a real -world 
challenge. Students are to apply the skills and knowledge learnt in their  curriculum so far, to 
ideate and deliver a working prototype by the end of the assignment. Students are to work in 
teams of 4/5 for this assignment, unless indicated otherwise by the tutor. 
The assignment will conclude with a ByteHackz to fast-track the development. 
2. CHAL
LENGE AREAS
In 
Week 1, students will be briefed about the given challenge statement. Following after that, 
students will undergo an activity that will result in formation of  teams of 4/5. From Week 2 to 
Week 5, each team is expected to go through the process from ideation to prototype 
development. 
Refer to the Challenge Statements document for details.
3. DELIVERABLES
a. Each team is required to give a pitch of their prototype on 20 Nov 2026 ( Fri) to your
tutor/industry Judges/ Peers. More details of the schedule will be provided nearer to
the date.
b. Each team submits all artefacts (i.e., prototyping files, code, presentation slides) by 19
Nov 2026 (Thu) 12pm to Brightspace.
