Centralised Student Management System 
 
Problem Statement: How can a centralised student management system improve the 
management of student enrolment, attendance, membership records, and training progress 
across multiple Aikido Shinjukai training centres? 
 
1. Problem Overview: 
Managing student records and activities across multiple Aikido Shinjukai training centres can be 
inefficient and challenging without a centralized system, leading to administrative difficulties and 
communication gaps. 
2. Objectives: 
Develop an all-in-one, mobile-responsive web-based platform to streamline Aikido Shinjukai 
centre operations, improve administrative efficiency and provide administrators, instructors and 
students with centralized access to relevant information. 
3. Scope: 
Key areas of focus: 
● Student registration, enrolment management and payment integration 
● Attendance tracking, monitoring and dashboard 
● Student training progress and grading records 
● Student data consolidation and reporting 
● Automated notifications 
● Student self-service portal 
 
Out of Scope: Detailed administrative operations, including grading result processing, belt 
promotion approvals and back-end administrative workflows. 
4. Technology Stack (Students are given the opportunity to explore alternative technologies): 
● Front-End: React.js / Next.js 
● Back-End: Node.js with Express 
● Database: PostgreSQL / MongoDB 
● Payment Gateway: Stripe

5. Expected Outcomes: 
A centralized platform that streamlines student management, reduces administrative workload 
and provides instructors with better visibility into student attendance and progression. Key 
outcomes include: 
1. Allow new students to register, create membership profiles automatically and receive 
automated welcome emails. Receive suitable class recommendations based on their 
belt levels, enroll in classes, make payments and receive registration confirmations – 
Student 
2. Record and monitor student attendance across multiple training centres, including 
external sessions, and identify students with declining attendance for timely follow-up – 
Administrator / Instructor 
3. Automatically assess students' grading eligibility based on predefined requirements, 
notify eligible students of upcoming grading opportunities and allow them to submit 
grading applications – Administrator → Student 
4. Allow instructors to assess students during scheduled grading sessions, record grading 
results, time in rank and feedback, and digitally sign and submit assessments for further 
processing – Instructor 
5. Provide a centralized dashboard for instructors to monitor student progression, 
attendance trends and grading readiness – Instructor 
6. Allow students to view their membership status, class schedules, grading applications 
and payment history through a self-service portal – Student
